One word for the whole internet — an entry for make.fun's The Internet in One Word bounty ($10, 1st $5 / 2nd $3 / 3rd $2).

## Entry

> wormhole

You click one link and fall through spacetime, emerging hours later somewhere utterly different. That is the whole internet.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) reads the word from `entry.txt` and writes it to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
